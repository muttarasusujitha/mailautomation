# Public Naukri job-seeker search

Naukri Search looks for public search-index results and accepts only links on
Naukri domains whose paths identify resume, candidate, or profile pages. It
does not search private Resdex records, open candidate pages, collect contact
details from those pages, or bypass login and access checks.

Public indexes may not expose candidate profiles for a given skill or location.
The app can return fewer than 10 profiles or none; it must not fill the target
with job listings, articles, fabricated profiles, or results from another site.
Private candidate resumes require recruiter access to Resdex.
